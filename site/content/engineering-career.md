An engineering career in electronics spans salaried employment, independent consulting and product ownership, with movement between them governed as much by labour-market structure and credentialing conventions as by technical capability. Falling entry into the profession compounds as the existing workforce ages, producing a persistent shortage that favours practising engineers and leaves employers competing for them.<sup><a href="#ref-54" data-tip="Ep 54: An Interview with Jack Ganssle - Embedded Elchee Epexegesis" title="Ep 54: An Interview with Jack Ganssle - Embedded Elchee Epexegesis">[54]</a></sup> Career planning changes shape with experience: a year or two out of school the plan is a specific job title to hold within five years, and after roughly ten years and several job changes that target is replaced by broader criteria such as being a capable and trusted resource.<sup><a href="#ref-670" data-tip="Ep 670: Engineering Careers with Circuit Break & James Lewis (June 14, 2024)" title="Ep 670: Engineering Careers with Circuit Break & James Lewis (June 14, 2024)">[670]</a></sup>

## Education and credentials

Mathematics taught as central to an engineering degree may go unused in practice, with more than twenty years in industry passing without a single occasion to use an integral, the applicability depending heavily on the area worked in.<sup><a href="#ref-33" data-tip="Ep 33: Bob Widlar, Electronic Design, FIRST Robotics - Monday, Meta Monday" title="Ep 33: Bob Widlar, Electronic Design, FIRST Robotics - Monday, Meta Monday">[33]</a></sup> An engineer returning to a university department after years in industry found the curriculum unchanged from his own student years, with the same topics, the same designs and the same textbooks.<sup><a href="#ref-302" data-tip="Ep 302: An Interview with Clint Cole of Digilent (June 8, 2016)" title="Ep 302: An Interview with Clint Cole of Digilent (June 8, 2016)">[302]</a></sup> Engineering students build their picture of industry from fellow students and academic staff alone, and academic staff are unrepresentative of working engineers, so the picture is distorted until direct contact with practising engineers corrects it.<sup><a href="#ref-81" data-tip="Ep 81: Jersey Jeff Jactitation (February 6, 2012)" title="Ep 81: Jersey Jeff Jactitation (February 6, 2012)">[81]</a></sup> Where a degree course is taught theoretically and leaves hands-on electronics outside the coursework, practical experience has to be obtained separately; in one case it came from working at Intel, Fujitsu and several startups concurrently with study.<sup><a href="#ref-549" data-tip="Ep 549: Creative Engineering with Shrouk El-Attar (July 11, 2021)" title="Ep 549: Creative Engineering with Shrouk El-Attar (July 11, 2021)">[549]</a></sup> Completing a combined university and company training programme does not guarantee technical work at the end of it either, since the positions offered were office roles whose day-to-day content had little to do with electrical engineering.<sup><a href="#ref-284" data-tip="Ep 284: An Interview with Great Scott (January 27, 2016)" title="Ep 284: An Interview with Great Scott (January 27, 2016)">[284]</a></sup>

A graduate degree can act as an admission filter to the applicant pool rather than as a requirement for the work itself, and in engineering a bachelor's degree is sufficient for most roles, whereas neighbouring sciences require a doctorate to be considered at all.<sup><a href="#ref-611" data-tip="Ep 611: Grad School Time Capsule with Joshua and Zach (December 4, 2022)" title="Ep 611: Grad School Time Capsule with Joshua and Zach (December 4, 2022)">[611]</a></sup> The absence of a degree cuts the other way. An unfinished degree, four years of electrical engineering stopped three courses short of completion, steered one career away from salaried employment and toward independent work on the assumption that employers would screen the missing credential out.<sup><a href="#ref-54" data-tip="Ep 54: An Interview with Jack Ganssle - Embedded Elchee Epexegesis" title="Ep 54: An Interview with Jack Ganssle - Embedded Elchee Epexegesis">[54]</a></sup> Entry into Silicon Valley engineering without any college degree has nonetheless been achieved through demonstrated delivery, by building a reputation as the person to approach to get a design finished.<sup><a href="#ref-394" data-tip="Ep 394: Jeri Ellsworth and the demise of CastAR (May 28, 2018)" title="Ep 394: Jeri Ellsworth and the demise of CastAR (May 28, 2018)">[394]</a></sup>

Access to the professional literature is itself tied to affiliation: the IEEE paper archive is normally bought by an institution, and an engineer without an academic or employer subscription faces a five-figure annual cost, so leaving that affiliation cuts off the literature.<sup><a href="#ref-4" data-tip="Ep 4: Cultural Differences" title="Ep 4: Cultural Differences">[4]</a></sup>

## Hiring and the labour market

Large companies hire new graduates readily and grow more reluctant as a candidate's experience accumulates, because the experienced applicant is more expensive and less easily directed, which is the basis of the route of taking the large-company job first for the training it provides and going independent later.<sup><a href="#ref-232" data-tip="Ep 232: Impedance Matching with Davidson and Vandenbout - Presbytes Pushing Portfolios" title="Ep 232: Impedance Matching with Davidson and Vandenbout - Presbytes Pushing Portfolios">[232]</a></sup> Graduates of the most prestigious engineering schools are largely absent from ordinary industry hiring pools, with none from Sydney's flagship university appearing across an entire career of interviewing in that city, because those graduates are recruited separately through campus job fairs by employers competing for them.<sup><a href="#ref-496" data-tip="Ep 496: Drab Olive (June 14, 2020)" title="Ep 496: Drab Olive (June 14, 2020)">[496]</a></sup>

Short tenures count against a candidate when a resume is read, so engineers commonly stay in a poorly fitting job for at least nine months to a year before moving.<sup><a href="#ref-215" data-tip="Ep 215: Wrong Hardware, Wrong Software - Fugacious Fan Funding (September 7, 2014)" title="Ep 215: Wrong Hardware, Wrong Software - Fugacious Fan Funding (September 7, 2014)">[215]</a></sup> Roughly five years spent in a university post or other work isolated from industrial practice is reported to make a candidate unattractive to industry employers, and a full-time content-creation record carries the same uncertainty when presented to a company.<sup><a href="#ref-284" data-tip="Ep 284: An Interview with Great Scott (January 27, 2016)" title="Ep 284: An Interview with Great Scott (January 27, 2016)">[284]</a></sup> In an interview the question asking a candidate to describe their ideal job is scored against the role on offer rather than treated as an open question.<sup><a href="#ref-256" data-tip="Ep 256: Is This A Show? (July 1, 2015)" title="Ep 256: Is This A Show? (July 1, 2015)">[256]</a></sup>

Electrical and firmware engineering forms a small community within any one region, and the same people recur across a career, which is the mechanism behind referral-driven hiring and the reason a badly ended relationship follows an engineer.<sup><a href="#ref-458" data-tip="Ep 458: An Interview with Ken Burns (September 15, 2019)" title="Ep 458: An Interview with Ken Burns (September 15, 2019)">[458]</a></sup> Technical support has been a common entry route into engineering roles, at SparkFun because support work demands deep product knowledge, with the consequence for the employer that the support team continually loses its best staff to engineering.<sup><a href="#ref-157" data-tip="Ep 157: An Interview with the SparkFun Team - Efficacious Engineering Ensemble (August 5, 2013)" title="Ep 157: An Interview with the SparkFun Team - Efficacious Engineering Ensemble (August 5, 2013)">[157]</a></sup>

Where an engineer wants to move into a different area of electronics, the question of whether further study will open the path recurs, and the answer given from years of fielding it is that changing employer accomplishes the move while additional coursework generally does not.<sup><a href="#ref-14" data-tip="Ep 14: China, Entrepreneurs and Blue Collar Reality" title="Ep 14: China, Entrepreneurs and Blue Collar Reality">[14]</a></sup>

### Skills and demand

Demand for one engineer's work changed when microcontroller programming was added to circuit building: discrete-logic and analog projects drew no paid requests, while the ability to program in assembly produced paid odd jobs and eventually an offer of employment.<sup><a href="#ref-194" data-tip="Ep 194: An Interview With Todd Bailey - Embedded Embrasure Engineering (April 14, 2014)" title="Ep 194: An Interview With Todd Bailey - Embedded Embrasure Engineering (April 14, 2014)">[194]</a></sup> Analog and integrated-circuit designers are both scarce and comparatively well paid, and a completed silicon design presented at interview is decisive portfolio evidence in that specialism.<sup><a href="#ref-15" data-tip="Ep 15: Analog Components, First Person Flying and Idea Ownership" title="Ep 15: Analog Components, First Person Flying and Idea Ownership">[15]</a></sup> PCB layout capability, by contrast, is becoming widely available, which improves access but weakens it as a differentiator when selling engineering services, with firmware judged to hold the harder remaining problems.<sup><a href="#ref-631" data-tip="Ep 631: A Noisy Rude Bus (May 7, 2023)" title="Ep 631: A Noisy Rude Bus (May 7, 2023)">[631]</a></sup>

Free or cheap design tools and low-cost prototyping have raised the output of a single engineer to what was previously ten people's work over the same period, which masks a workforce shortfall rather than removing it.<sup><a href="#ref-302" data-tip="Ep 302: An Interview with Clint Cole of Digilent (June 8, 2016)" title="Ep 302: An Interview with Clint Cole of Digilent (June 8, 2016)">[302]</a></sup> The United States responds to its engineering shortfall by importing engineers and exporting jobs, but the same shortage is reported in Australia and Western Europe, so the labour is not simply relocating between regions.<sup><a href="#ref-302" data-tip="Ep 302: An Interview with Clint Cole of Digilent (June 8, 2016)" title="Ep 302: An Interview with Clint Cole of Digilent (June 8, 2016)">[302]</a></sup>

## Compensation

United States compensation as stated around 2011 had a senior engineer earning a six-figure salary against a median family income of $51,000 for an entire household, while in Australia six figures was attainable but marked out an engineer as doing well rather than typical.<sup><a href="#ref-54" data-tip="Ep 54: An Interview with Jack Ganssle - Embedded Elchee Epexegesis" title="Ep 54: An Interview with Jack Ganssle - Embedded Elchee Epexegesis">[54]</a></sup> An Australian engineering pay slip from about twenty years before 2011 recorded an hourly rate of $11.6396.<sup><a href="#ref-56" data-tip="Ep 56: Open Orbific Oratiuncle" title="Ep 56: Open Orbific Oratiuncle">[56]</a></sup> Degreed engineers with around three years of experience expect between $90,000 and $200,000 a year, which places board-level consumer repair below the wage they will accept and leaves that work to trained non-engineers earning $40,000 to $50,000.<sup><a href="#ref-311" data-tip="Ep 311: An Interview with Louis Rossmann (August 10, 2016)" title="Ep 311: An Interview with Louis Rossmann (August 10, 2016)">[311]</a></sup>

Sales roles are paid a commission on each unit sold while the engineer who designed the product has no equivalent share of its revenue, so earning materially more than a salary from engineering work requires owning and selling the product rather than being paid to design it.<sup><a href="#ref-5" data-tip="Ep 5: Girl Power" title="Ep 5: Girl Power">[5]</a></sup>

A reported 40 percent of women holding engineering degrees either leave the profession or never enter it; no comparable figure for men was available, and a control group is needed before the number can be interpreted.<sup><a href="#ref-213" data-tip="Ep 213: Travel Recaps and Altium Announcements - Artisinal Aussie Assemblage" title="Ep 213: Travel Recaps and Altium Announcements - Artisinal Aussie Assemblage">[213]</a></sup>

## Working conditions

Workload expectations differ sharply between engineering employers and circulate by reputation within a local industry, with the difference appearing in hours worked rather than in output delivered.<sup><a href="#ref-215" data-tip="Ep 215: Wrong Hardware, Wrong Software - Fugacious Fan Funding (September 7, 2014)" title="Ep 215: Wrong Hardware, Wrong Software - Fugacious Fan Funding (September 7, 2014)">[215]</a></sup> Toy design at one employer ran at roughly eighty-hour weeks sustained over several years.<sup><a href="#ref-194" data-tip="Ep 194: An Interview With Todd Bailey - Embedded Embrasure Engineering (April 14, 2014)" title="Ep 194: An Interview With Todd Bailey - Embedded Embrasure Engineering (April 14, 2014)">[194]</a></sup> Defence work carries documentation and procurement overhead that consumes the engineer's time in place of design, with two weeks spent specifying a single screw given as representative.<sup><a href="#ref-491" data-tip="Ep 491: The Almighty Dollarydoo (May 3, 2020)" title="Ep 491: The Almighty Dollarydoo (May 3, 2020)">[491]</a></sup>

In-house product development frequently ends in cancellation, so an employed engineer may spend six months or more on work that never reaches a market, and the stated reason for one departure from employment was that independent work produces output that actually ships.<sup><a href="#ref-140" data-tip="Ep 140: Project Management, Lasers & Robots - Staunch Specialty Sanctanimity (April 8, 2013)" title="Ep 140: Project Management, Lasers & Robots - Staunch Specialty Sanctanimity (April 8, 2013)">[140]</a></sup> Work prepared before it is formally requested lets an engineer deliver immediately when the request arrives, and internal standing at an employer is set by a small number of such visible events and persists for the remainder of the engineer's time at that company.<sup><a href="#ref-116" data-tip="Ep 116: Distribution, Wozniak & Robots - Early Eight-bit Endgame (October 7, 2012)" title="Ep 116: Distribution, Wozniak & Robots - Early Eight-bit Endgame (October 7, 2012)">[116]</a></sup>

Taking electronics as both hobby and occupation removed the hobby in one account, because the working day consisted of the same activity that had been the recreation.<sup><a href="#ref-435" data-tip="Ep 435: An Interview with Andreas Spiess (March 24, 2019)" title="Ep 435: An Interview with Andreas Spiess (March 24, 2019)">[435]</a></sup>

## Progression and transitions

Engineers move into management to gain control over decisions they watch being made badly, and the move trades away the hands-on technical work that motivated it.<sup><a href="#ref-140" data-tip="Ep 140: Project Management, Lasers & Robots - Staunch Specialty Sanctanimity (April 8, 2013)" title="Ep 140: Project Management, Lasers & Robots - Staunch Specialty Sanctanimity (April 8, 2013)">[140]</a></sup> Running a funded company likewise means taking on the work of raising money, which cannot be combined with continuing to practise engineering.<sup><a href="#ref-267" data-tip="Ep 267: Standing With Ahmed (September 16, 2015)" title="Ep 267: Standing With Ahmed (September 16, 2015)">[267]</a></sup> A company that completed a commercial product still failed to sell it because none of the people running it could sell, and the missing commercial function rather than the technology ended the business.<sup><a href="#ref-424" data-tip="Ep 424: An Interview with Julia Truchsess (January 6, 2019)" title="Ep 424: An Interview with Julia Truchsess (January 6, 2019)">[424]</a></sup>

Independence has its own preconditions. Clients hire consultants on the strength of accumulated employed experience, so consulting is not an entry-level path, and experience of both large and small organisations broadens what the consultant can offer.<sup><a href="#ref-588" data-tip="Ep 588: Siloed Engineering with Leigh Brady (May 8, 2022)" title="Ep 588: Siloed Engineering with Leigh Brady (May 8, 2022)">[588]</a></sup> A consulting practice can nonetheless start as moonlighting alongside employment: two years of part-time work for a single client, carried out with the employer's knowledge, preceded one move to full time, with the work priced well below its market rate throughout that period.<sup><a href="#ref-624" data-tip="Ep 624: Design & Manufacturing Consulting with Scott Williams from Xentronics" title="Ep 624: Design & Manufacturing Consulting with Scott Williams from Xentronics">[624]</a></sup> The practice does not sustain itself without time allocated to it, and a consulting side business goes dormant when its principal holds a full-time job and is also studying.<sup><a href="#ref-583" data-tip="Ep 583: The Smart Grid with Paul Zawada (March 27, 2022)" title="Ep 583: The Smart Grid with Paul Zawada (March 27, 2022)">[583]</a></sup> The test proposed for going independent early in a career, around the five-year mark, is whether mentors and people to learn from remain reachable outside an employer, since access to them is the main thing employment otherwise supplies.<sup><a href="#ref-541" data-tip="Ep 541: Chip Shortage Denier (May 10, 2021)" title="Ep 541: Chip Shortage Denier (May 10, 2021)">[541]</a></sup> Choosing career moves primarily by the people available to learn from is offered as the most reliable single criterion, explicitly qualified as coming from someone late in career with choices available, since a person starting out who needs the income takes the income first.<sup><a href="#ref-701" data-tip="Ep 701: Electric Propulsion with Todd Bailey (August 21, 2025)" title="Ep 701: Electric Propulsion with Todd Bailey (August 21, 2025)">[701]</a></sup>

Self-employment is not automatically an escape from salaried hours. Hand-packing and shipping kits maximises profit per unit but converts the business into a twelve-hour-a-day job with substantial revenue and little margin.<sup><a href="#ref-142" data-tip="Ep 142: Kickstarter, IndieGoGo & Ignite - Jasperated Jimswinger Jobbery (April 22, 2013)" title="Ep 142: Kickstarter, IndieGoGo & Ignite - Jasperated Jimswinger Jobbery (April 22, 2013)">[142]</a></sup>
